How it works

From dream to roadmap
in under 5 minutes

1
Tell Pharos your dream
Type what you want to become — a developer, designer, data scientist, anything. Pharos asks a few questions to understand your timeline and budget.
2
Get your personal roadmap
Pharos generates a structured, multi-phase learning path with curated resources, milestones, and estimated durations — all tailored to you.
